03 · Key issues in IS & digital transformation

Issue 1 · Leaks got small问题一：泄密变"小"了

Then: gigabytes, caught.
Now: kilobytes, missed.过去：几个 GB，抓得到。现在：几 KB，抓不到。
To see a 200-line paste, you'd have to read content: the biggest privacy cost.想看到 200 行代码的粘贴，就得看内容，而这是最大的隐私代价。

03 · Key issues in IS & digital transformation

Issues 3–6 · What the design must solve问题三到六：设计必须解决的事

Pseudonymise, don't anonymise假名化，不是匿名化

Hidden by default, traceable after an alert.平时看不到是谁，报警后能追到人。

Sort by destination按目的地分类

Off-limits: banks, health, private chat. Watched: AI, cloud drives, code hosts.不碰：银行、医疗、私聊。重点看：AI、网盘、代码托管。

Blind spots盲区

Phones, home Wi-Fi, photos of a screen.手机、家里的网络、拍屏幕。

Baselines are AI decisions个人基线就是自动化决策

Per-person baselines need a risk assessment (California ADMT).每人一条基线，须做风险评估（加州 ADMT）。
